Research Highlights

Key contributions and innovations

🤖 Thought of Search (ToS)

Novel approach for planning with language models through the lens of efficiency.

🔗 Blog Post 📄 NeurIPS 2024 📄 ICAPS 2026

🧠 Generalized Planning with LLMs

Improved generalized planning via pseudocode strategy refinement and reflection, achieving 82% average coverage across 17 PDDL domains.

💻 GitHub 📄 ICAPS 2026

🔍 Fact-Level Relevance Pruning

Aggressive task simplification by reasoning about relevance at the level of facts rather than variables, preserving all shortest optimal plans.

💻 GitHub 📄 ICAPS 2026

📡 GNN-Based Planner Selection

Heterogeneous Graph Neural Networks (RGCN+XGBoost) for online planner selection, achieving 91.7% accuracy — improving over prior methods.

📄 ICAPS 2026

📢 LLM-Based Planning: Position Papers

Two position papers on the state and future of planning in the LLM era: examining planner-generation methods (NL2Search, NL2PDDL, NL2Policy) and calling for rigorous methodology grounded in six decades of planning community insights.

📄 ICAPS 2026 📄 ICML 2026

📊 ACPBench

Comprehensive benchmark for reasoning about action, change, and planning with language models.

🔗 Blog Post 📄 AAAI 2025 📄 ICLR 2026

🌈 Diverse Planning

Techniques for generating diverse sets of plans, providing users with meaningful alternatives and flexibility in decision-making.

📄 AAAI 2020 📄 AAAI 2022 📄 ICAPS 2022

🎯 Top-Quality Planning

Methods for finding practically useful sets of best plans, enabling better decision-making in planning applications.

📄 ICAPS 2018 📄 AAAI 2020 📄 ICAPS 2022 📄 IJCAI 2023 📄 SoCS 2023a 📄 SoCS 2023b 📄 SoCS 2024 📄 ICAPS 2024

🔴⚫ Red-Black Planning

Systematic approach to partial delete relaxation, forming the basis of award-winning planners Mercury and Cerberus.

📄 AAAI 2013 📄 ICAPS 2013 📄 SoCS 2013 📄 HSDIP 2014 📄 AIJ 2015 📄 AAAI 2019 📄 IJCAI 2021

🏆 Abstraction Heuristics

Implicit abstraction heuristics for cost-optimal planning. Winner of ICAPS Best Dissertation Award 2011 and Influential Paper Award 2023.

📄 PhD Thesis 📄 ICAPS 2008

🔄 Planning & RL

Bridging automated planning and reinforcement learning for autonomous systems and hybrid approaches.

📄 ICAPS 2021a 📄 IJCAI 2023a 📄 ICAPS 2024 📄 ICAPS 2022 📄 NeurIPS 2023 📄 IJCAI 2023b 📄 IJCAI 2021

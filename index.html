Research Focus

Advancing AI through automated planning and language models

🤖

AI Planning & LLMs

Current Focus

Developing reliable and efficient methods for planning with large language models. Recent work includes Thought of Search (ToS) and AutoToS for automated planning.

🎯

Automated Planning

Core Expertise

Classical planning, heuristic search, abstraction techniques, and structural patterns. Winner of IPC 2018 cost-optimal track with Delfi planner.

🔄

Planning & RL Integration

Hybrid Approaches

Bridging automated planning and reinforcement learning techniques for autonomous systems. Co-creator of the PRL workshop series.
